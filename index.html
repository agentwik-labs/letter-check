What does this letter want from me?

Paste the words from any letter, bill, notice or text message. See the deadline, the amount, what to do, what happens if you don't, and scam warning signs.

🔒 Runs on your device. Nothing you paste is sent anywhere.
Only have the paper letter?

iPhone: open the Camera, point it at the letter, tap the text icon in the corner, then Copy All.
Android: open Google Lens (or the camera's Lens button), tap Text, select all, then Copy.
Then come back and paste here.

Want it explained in full, in your language?

Copy this and paste it into any free AI chat (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot). We removed the names, Social Security numbers, account numbers, emails, phone numbers and street addresses we could find. Read it before you paste and delete anything else personal.

Not legal, medical, tax or financial advice. This tool reads the words with simple rules. It can miss a date, pick the wrong one, or miss a scam. Always check the deadline on the letter itself, and call the sender using a number from their official website.
